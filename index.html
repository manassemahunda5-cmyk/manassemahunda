How I work

Design,
then ship

Step 1

Understand the product

Start with the people, the goal and the constraints, then map the system before drawing a single screen.

Step 2

Structure and flow

Turn the map into flows and wireframes that make the product easy to move through.

Step 3

Design with intent

Type, colour, spacing and motion chosen for the product, tuned until it looks good and reads clearly.

Step 4

Prototype with AI

Use AI workflows to get to a clickable prototype fast, so ideas get tested sooner.
